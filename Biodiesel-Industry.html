Biodiesel Industry

NEW ERA recognizes the growing demand for environmentally friendly fuel solutions. Bio-diesel, a readily available liquid biofuel processed from renewable sources like vegetable oils and animal fats, offers a compelling alternative to traditional fuels.

Easy Handling and Storage:

Biodiesel boasts safe handling and storage characteristics, making it a practical choice for various applications.

Environmentally Friendly:

Its biodegradability and non-toxic nature minimize environmental impact in case of spills.

Cleaner Burning:

The presence of oxygen in bio-diesel allows for more efficient combustion, resulting in significantly reduced emissions of harmful pollutants.

Reduced Sulfur and PAHs:

Biodiesel is free of sulphur and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), contributing to cleaner air and improved public health.
