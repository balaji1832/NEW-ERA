Ceramic, Stone &
Marble Industry

NEW ERA pumps are a trusted choice for various applications within the ceramic industry, ensuring efficient and reliable performance

Precise Additive Dosing:

Need to add colorants, lime or dual-component resins to concrete mixes? Our pumps provide accurate and controlled dosing guaranteeing a consistent final product.

Efficient Concrete Pumping:

Move concrete with ease. Our pumps are designed for smooth and reliable concrete transfer minimizing downtime and maximizing productivity.

Drilling Machine Support:

Our pumps efficiently supply drilling machines, optimizing drilling operations and contributing to faster project completion.

Benefits of Ceramic, Stone & Marble Industry

Colorants : Achieve consistent color throughout your ceramic products.

Lime : Our pumps ensure smooth handling of lime for optimal results.

Dual-Component Resins : We offer solutions for precise metering and mixing of dual-component resins.

Water and Sand : Efficiently transfer water and sand mixtures for your ceramic production needs.

Suspended Glass Powder : Our pumps are equipped to handle even challenging materials like suspended glass powder.

Regardless of your specific needs in the ceramic industry NEW ERA has the perfect pumping solution for you. Contact us today to discuss your application and discover how our pumps can optimize your production process.

Some of the common pumping applications are abrasive fluids like glaze, slip and colour etc.
