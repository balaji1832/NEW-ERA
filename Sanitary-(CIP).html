Sanitary (CIP) Solutions

New Era’s pumping solutions are designed to meet the requirements of sterile and shear-sensitive fluids. They are recommended for applications in the beverage, brewery, food and other sanitary environments. Featuring quick knock-down capabilities, these pumps offer enhanced ease of cleaning, thanks to their smooth, flat surfaces with no recessed areas where bacteria can hide. Additionally, they provide the advantage of reduced downtime during regular maintenance, fewer moving parts, and the use of the highest quality materials.
